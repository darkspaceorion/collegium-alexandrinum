# Agent roster

Greek title in the app, original name from the build-out PDF, what it does, and when the Dean (the router) calls it.

| # | Title | PDF name | Job | Called when | Version | Runs on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assessor | Interviewer | True reason, baseline, 3-level check | New course | v1 | App, default tier |
| 2 | Cartographer | Map Maker | Builds the curriculum and course pack, sends out the scouts | Assessment done | v1 | NEXUS desktop (`/collegium build`) |
| 3 | Scholar | Explainer ("Professor") | Teaches a block. Read, set up, solve, check, then redo with no help | Block not yet taught, or the student is stuck or failed | v1 | App, default tier |
| 4 | Dialectician | Socratic Questioner | Why and how questions, never gives the answer | "I get it", end of module, pop quiz | v2 | App |
| 5 | Proctor | Examiner | Quizzes that get harder, concept-tagged | Block taught | v1 | App, default to write, quick to mark |
| 6 | Logician | Checker | Checks the reasoning in work the student made | Student submits work, or the same topic is missed on several quizzes | v2 | App |
| 7 | Auditor | Listener | Compares a spoken explanation to the course pack | Before moving on, pop quiz, "can I explain..." | v2 | App, phone dictation |
| 8 | Diagnostician | Diagnostician | Finds the root gap behind repeated misses | Same concept missed twice or more, before a module closes | v2 | App |
| 9 | Challenger | Sparring Partner | Timed role-play against the goal, difficulty slider | Skill finished, course done, "help me prep for..." | v2 | App |
| 10 | Registrar | Clerk | Files notes and results, makes cards from misses, schedule, reminders | After every quiz and review, session end | v1 | App, quick tier |

**Not one of the 10: the scouts.** 1-3 Haiku agents the Cartographer sends out once, at curriculum build, to check that the material is current.

**Tools (p16), for later:** role-play, realistic-task, practice-app.
