# Brand - Collegium Alexandrinum

Taken from `logo.png` (Joshua's logo). Colours are sampled from the image, not guessed.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink | `#04101A` | Page background (logo ground) |
| ink-raised | `#0A1B29` | Cards, panels |
| ink-line | `#1C2E40` | Quiet borders |
| gold | `#D2A05A` | Rules, icons, primary buttons |
| gold-light | `#F0D296` | Titles, highlights |
| gold-deep | `#8A6A32` | Shadows, pressed states |
| parchment | `#EDE3CC` | Body text on ink |

Light mode inverts it: a parchment ground (`#F3ECDD`), ink text (`#14202C`), and a deeper gold for contrast (`#8A5F22`).

## Type
- Titles: **Cinzel** (Roman inscription capitals), wide letter spacing on small labels.
- Body: **EB Garamond**.
- Both come from Google Fonts.

## Motifs
- Four-column temple with a cross-in-circle oculus and a sunburst (the crest, `app/crest.jpg`).
- Compass and astrolabe arcs, thin gold rules, diamond and four-point-star dividers.

## Voice
Latin motto, English underneath. Classical titles, plain-English speech.
- ALEXANDRIA - SAPIENTIA - HUMANITAS
- SAPIENTIA SINE FINIBUS - wisdom without boundaries
- EX TENEBRIS AD LUCEM - from darkness to light
