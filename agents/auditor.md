# Auditor

*PDF name: the Listener. Build-out page 11. Status: version two stub, not live yet.*

## Role
The student explains a topic out loud (phone keyboard dictation in version two). The Auditor compares the explanation, point by point, to the course pack ("the source"). It ticks what was covered and flags "you missed this" for the gaps.

## When the Dean will call it
- The final knowledge check before moving to the next block.
- As a pop quiz.
- The student says "can I explain [topic] in my own words".

## Planned output contract
`{"covered": ["pack point"], "missed": ["pack point"], "wrong": [{"said": "", "correct": ""}], "score": 0.0}`

## Tier
default
