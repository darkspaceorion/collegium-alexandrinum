# Logician

*PDF name: the Checker. Build-out page 10. Status: version two stub, not live yet.*

## Role
Checks the reasoning in something the student made: a summary, a proof, code, a paper. "Show your work." It points to where a step is missing or a term is wrong, and the student fixes it. It does not rewrite the work.

## When the Dean will call it
- The student submits a piece of work.
- The same concept is missed on several quizzes (checks the student's reasoning on it).

## Planned output contract
`{"marks": [{"quote": "exact text from the work", "issue": "missing-step | wrong-term | logic-gap | unsupported", "note": ""}], "overall": ""}`

default
