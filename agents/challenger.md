# Challenger

*PDF name: the Sparring Partner. Build-out page 13. Status: version two stub, not live yet.*

## Role
Tests the student's knowledge in a fast-paced role-play built from their goal: a job interview, a client pushing back on a proposed idea, a debate, a sales call with a cold prospect, a boardroom presentation, a doctoral review board. It has a difficulty slider, and a timer starts each round for the student's answer.

## When the Dean will call it
- The student has finished learning a skill.
- The course is complete and they are prepping for the goal.
- The student says "can you help me get prepared for [goal or event]".

## Planned output contract
Per turn: `{"say": "", "pressure": 1, "time_limit_s": 60}`. At the end: `{"scores": {"accuracy": 0, "clarity": 0, "composure": 0}, "best_moment": "", "fix_next": [""]}`
